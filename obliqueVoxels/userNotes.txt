Okay!  The smoothing is generally working MUCH better now.  The normals make sense, when inspected visually.


However, I previously suggested that "boundary" smoothed faces should not be smoothed at all.  As you pointed out in your notes, this means that the rims of thin rings cannot be smoothed.  This is actually incorrect behavior.  The boundary faces should still be smoothed!   But they should only consider other smoothed, connected faces in their basket for averaging, and ignore neighboring faces that are not smoothed.

There's an example called "rimNotSmoothed.ovox" that has a thin rectangular shape with just the rim smoothed.   Several versions ago, those edge faces would get their normals rotated, but now that doesn't happen.
