PATH MASTER

A single-player roguelike auto-walker and auto-fighter.  The wizard walks
the path and fights on his own.  You control only his build:  the web of
gems inside his wand.


BUILDING

    ./build.sh

Pure C89, compiled with GCC.  Links only SDL 2 and OpenGL (and libm).
All pixel art, the pixel font, the sound effects, and the music are
generated by the program itself at startup.  There are no asset files.


PLAYING

    ./pathMaster

Mouse only.

  - Drag gems from the satchel into the open sockets of the wand's web.
  - Every gem changes the wand (hover over one to read what it does).
  - Two different gems joined by a thread of the web may form a synergy,
    which grants an extra power.  The thread lights up when they do.
    Hovering over a gem lists its synergy partners.
  - Two gems of the same kind joined by a thread resonate (+25% power).
    Opals empower every gem they touch.
  - Drop a gem onto its twin (same kind, same level) to fuse them into
    one stronger gem.  This works in the web and in the satchel.
  - Web Shards open sealed sockets.  Drag one onto a sealed socket that
    touches an open one.
  - Drop spare gems into the crucible to melt them into experience.
  - Each level gained offers a choice of three boons.
  - Every eighth wave is a boss.  The path never ends;  see how far
    you can get.  Your best run is remembered in pathMasterBest.txt.

Buttons at the top right:  pause, speed x1, x2, x4, sound (all / effects
only / silent), fullscreen, quit.  The wand can be rearranged while
paused.

The 640x480 picture is scaled by the largest whole number that fits the
window or screen, with black bars around it, so pixels stay sharp.


SOURCE

    main.c    window, scaling, main loop, test modes
    logic.c   the whole simulation (no video needed)
    ui.c      drawing and mouse control
    gfx.c     sprite atlas, primitives, the UTF-8 pixel font
    art.c     pixel art as text, gem shading, generated scenery
    sound.c   synthesizer for effects and music
    text.c    every user-visible string (for translation)
    game.h    shared declarations

All text is UTF-8 and goes through T() in text.c, so a translation is one
more string table.  The font covers ASCII and Latin-1 (accented letters
are composed from base letters and marks in gfx.c).


TESTING

    ./pathMaster --sim 50 --seed 1

runs 50 games with no window, played by a simple scripted player, and
prints how far each one got.  Use this to check balance after changing
numbers in logic.c.

    ./pathMaster --autoplay --speed 4 --seed 7

shows the scripted player in a window.  To watch without disturbing the
real desktop:

    Xvfb :97 -screen 0 1400x1050x24 &
    DISPLAY=:97 SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy ./pathMaster --autoplay &
    DISPLAY=:97 scrot shot.png

xdotool can click and drag in that display (mousemove, mousedown 1,
mouseup 1).  --fonttest shows a page of sample text, accents included.
